01 // Clinical Specialties & Gold-Standard Protocols

Specialized care for the demands of contemporary adult life.

Gold-Standard CBT Protocol

Anxiety Disorders & Panic Disorder

How symptoms show up:

Excessive, uncontrollable worry about the future, racing thoughts at night, chest tightness, rapid heartbeat, fear of losing control, and avoiding places or situations.

Practical CBT approach:

Cognitive restructuring of catastrophic thoughts, autonomic and breathing regulation techniques, gradual interoceptive exposure, and reduction of safety behaviors.

Typical process duration: 12 to 24 structured sessions
Executive Health & Career

Burnout, Corporate Stress & Overwork

How symptoms show up:

Physical and mental exhaustion that does not improve over the weekend, cynicism or emotional detachment from work, guilt when resting, rigid perfectionism, and imposter feelings.

Practical CBT approach:

Reframing hyper-demanding beliefs, assertiveness training to set workplace boundaries without guilt, sleep hygiene, and reclaiming everyday sources of fulfillment.

Typical process duration: 16 to 28 sessions + prevention
Executive Functions

Adult ADHD & Attention Regulation

How symptoms show up:

Chronic procrastination on important tasks, paralysis from overstimulation, trouble managing deadlines, verbal or financial impulsivity, and eroded self-esteem.

Practical CBT approach:

Safren's (Harvard) CBT protocol for adult ADHD: external organization systems, goal chunking, distractibility management, and healing lifelong self-criticism.

Typical process duration: 16 to 24 training sessions
Activation & Meaning

Depression, Grief & Major Life Transitions

How symptoms show up:

Loss of interest or pleasure (anhedonia), morning apathy, appetite or sleep shifts, emptiness after bereavement, divorce, or relocating abroad (migratory grief).

Practical CBT approach:

Gradual Behavioral Activation, compassionate grief processing, reframing the negative cognitive triad, and rebuilding a routine aligned with your core values.

Typical process duration: 20 to 36 ongoing sessions
Schema Therapy & CBT

Relationships, Attachment & Self-Care

How symptoms show up:

Chronic difficulty saying "no", fear of rejection or abandonment, emotional dependency, repeatedly choosing unavailable partners, and suppressing your own needs.

Practical CBT approach:

Mapping early maladaptive schemas, nonviolent assertive communication, strengthening grounded self-esteem, and building secure, reciprocal relationships.

Typical process duration: 16 to 30 sessions

02 // Transparent Therapeutic Journey

How the Process Works in Practice

In Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, you are never left wondering where treatment is heading. We work in collaborative partnership, with clear goals from intake to discharge.

01 Sessions 1–2

Initial Intake Session

An unhurried 50-minute conversation to listen to your current story, understand what brought you here, ease immediate distress, and answer questions about the method.

✓ Empathetic rapport and strict confidentiality
02 Formulation

Personalized Treatment Plan

Together we map your emotional patterns (triggers, automatic thoughts, and core cycles) and define concrete objectives you want to achieve.

✓ Clear, measurable goals
03 Intervention

Weekly Practical Tools

Therapy continues between sessions too. You receive manageable practical exercises, readings, and emotional regulation strategies applicable to your work and relationships.

✓ Exclusive digital support materials
04 Consolidation

Clinical Discharge & Autonomy

CBT aims to help you become your own therapist. Once goals are met, we gradually space out sessions (biweekly/monthly) toward a confident discharge.

✓ Long-term relapse prevention
