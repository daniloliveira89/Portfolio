[ TEXTILE ENGINEERING SPECIFICATIONS ]

ANATOMY OF A 480 GSM GARMENT

SPEC // 01 DENSITY

400 to 480 GSM Weight

While conventional labels rely on 160g jersey, we knit our tees in 400 GSM and hoodies in 480 GSM 3-end fleece—creating architectural structure that never clings or loses shape.

SPEC // 02 RAW MATERIAL

Pima & Egyptian Cotton

Cold-combed extra-long staple (ELS) fibers that prevent pilling, deliver a smooth brushed interior feel, and ensure superior thermal breathability.

SPEC // 03 CONSTRUCTION

Flatlock Seams & 3.5cm Collar

Industrial 5-thread seams reinforced at the shoulders and armholes. High-recovery 2x2 ribbed collar that holds a crisp neckline after hundreds of wears.

SPEC // 04 FINISHING

Stone-Washed Dye & Pre-Shrinking

Matte carbon mineral bath followed by enzyme washing and controlled tumble drying. 0% residual shrinkage after domestic laundering.
