02 // Constructive Philosophy & Tactile Soul

Visual silence, millimeter precision, and respect for the life of the tree.

We believe true furniture endures across generations. In the Japandi tradition — a synthesis of warm Nordic functionality (*Hygge*) and the Japanese aesthetic of natural imperfection (*Wabi-Sabi*) — every solid timber slab is carefully read by our master cabinetmakers before the first cut.

We never use MDF, synthetic veneers, or plasticized polyurethane varnishes. The wood breathes, develops a living patina, and preserves its original scent and grain.

STAGE 01 // RAW MATERIAL FSC® C148291

FSC®-Certified Solid Hardwood

Select logs from sustainable forest management in the Legal Amazon (Freijó and Cumaru) and certified temperate forests (Oak and Walnut). Slow kiln drying controlled at 9% moisture content for absolute dimensional stability.

Origin Traceability via QR Code
STAGE 02 // STRUCTURE SASHIMONO JOINERY

Traditional Nail-Free Joinery

We craft through-tenon, dovetail, half-lap, and contrasting hardwood wedge-lock joints. Each piece flexes mechanically as a single living organism with zero metal fatigue.

Lifetime Structural Warranty
STAGE 03 // TACTILE ALCHEMY ZERO VOC

Natural Oil & Carnauba Wax Finish

Hand-sanding up to 600 grit followed by deep nourishment with cold-pressed pure tung oil and Brazilian carnauba plant wax. Preserves open pores and the velvety feel of living wood.

Annual Care & Nourishment Kit Included
